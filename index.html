About

I am a BIM (Building Information Modeling) engineer with 6+ years of industry experience at China Construction Eighth Engineering Bureau, where I lead MEP BIM work for large-scale commercial, healthcare and infrastructure projects, and pioneered prefabricated (DfMA) chiller-plant delivery — from parametric family libraries and pump-module design to factory coordination and on-site supervision.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities (Fall 2026). My goal is to bring first-principles, learning-based approaches into engineering design and construction — a domain where design knowledge is still largely hand-crafted and human-heuristic-driven.

Research Interests

  • AI for the built environment — learning-based approaches to engineering design, BIM automation, and construction intelligence.
  • Self-taught systems that go beyond human heuristics — inspired by AlphaZero-style learning: systems that, starting from first principles rather than human experience, discover solutions and knowledge unknown to human experts.
  • Design space exploration — search, optimization and generative methods for MEP layout, prefabrication and modular design.
